Bring your projects, tasks, and notes together in one calm, colorful workspace — on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Download on the App Store

What is notal?

notal helps you organize what's notable. It's not just another notes app — it's a calm project workspace where your tasks and notes live together, under the projects they belong to. Folders hold projects, projects hold documents, and every document keeps your to-dos and your notes side by side.

Everything syncs across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac through iCloud. No ads.

Plan your time

  • Tasks with due dates, times, reminders, and repeats
  • A Today view for what needs you right now
  • Day and Week calendars to see the bigger picture
  • Overdue and completed tasks, neatly grouped

Write, richly

  • Rich text sections: bold, italic, underline, fonts, sizes, and colors
  • Bulleted lists and left / center / right alignment
  • Tables with per-cell formatting and alignment
  • Link text to a web page — or to any section in any document, and jump straight to it

Focus on what matters

  • Flag a task, event, or section as a priority and it rises to the top
  • Pin the things you want to keep an eye on to your Pinned board
  • A Scratchpad that's always one tap away

Made to feel right

  • iCloud keeps everything in sync across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac
  • Colorful, customizable blocks with automatic contrast so text stays readable
  • Full light and dark mode
  • Dates, times, and numbers always follow your device's regional settings
  • A clean, modern design that stays out of your way

Free to try

Use notal free for a month. After that, keep everything with a monthly or annual subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. No ads, ever.
