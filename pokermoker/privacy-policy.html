English

Summary

PokerMoker is a card-game app you play against the computer for fun, with play-money chips that have no real-world value. There are no accounts, and we collect no personal data on our own servers — we don't run any. Your game progress stays on your device. The app is free and supported by ads, so it includes Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising data to show you ads. You control ad consent, and any in-app purchase removes the banner ads for good.

Who is responsible

PokerMoker is made by NAE Dev. For any privacy question, contact nae_dev@icloud.com.

Your game data stays on your device

Your chip balance, game statistics, language and appearance settings, and other preferences are stored locally on your device. We have no servers and never receive, see, or store this data. One small flag — whether you have removed ads — is saved to Apple's iCloud key-value store so that, if you buy on one device, ads stay removed on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. That flag carries no personal information about you.

No account, no sign-up

PokerMoker has no login or account of its own. You can start playing immediately, offline if you like.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free app shows banner ads and optional rewarded videos (watch a short video to earn chips) through Google AdMob. To select, deliver, and measure these ads, Google may process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and settings, and your interactions with the ads. Google acts as an independent controller of that data under its own Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We never receive your amounts, gameplay content, or identity through ads.

Your consent choices (UMP / GDPR)

Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the first time you open the app we show Google's consent form so you can choose how your data is used for ads, including whether ads are personalized. You can change your choices at any time in Settings → About → Privacy choices.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS, before the advertising identifier can be used to personalize ads, the system asks for your permission with Apple's tracking prompt. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can change this any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Removing ads

Any in-app purchase permanently removes the banner ads. Once ads are removed, the banner no longer loads. Rewarded videos remain available and free if you ever want to earn extra chips.

Purchases

Chip packs are purchased through the App Store using Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment or card details.

Measurement (SKAdNetwork)

The app supports Apple's SKAdNetwork, a privacy-preserving system that lets advertisers measure ad campaigns without identifying you.

Children's privacy

PokerMoker is rated 18+ for simulated gambling and is not directed at children. It contains no real-money gambling, and we collect no personal data from anyone.

Security

Your game data is protected by the security of your device (Apple device encryption). Because we store none of your data on our own systems, there is nothing on our side to be exposed.

International data transfers

Google and Apple may process ad and purchase requests on servers in other countries, under their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email nae_dev@icloud.com.
