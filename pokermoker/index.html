What is PokerMoker?

PokerMoker is a friendly collection of 20 card games in one app — from favorites like Poker Hold'em, Blackjack and Video Poker to classics like Gin Rummy, Hearts, Solitaire and regional games loved around the world. Play solo against fair computer opponents, any time, even offline.

Every game is play-to-win — make the best hand, take the most tricks, or clear the board. There's nothing to wager and nothing to buy in order to play.

Rated 4+. Just card games — no wagering of any kind.
