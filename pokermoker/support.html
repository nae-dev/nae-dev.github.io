Frequently asked questions

Is this real gambling? Can I win real money?

No. PokerMoker is for entertainment only. You play with virtual chips that have no real-world value — they can't be cashed out, redeemed, or transferred, and there are no cash prizes. The app is rated 18+ because it simulates casino games.

I ran out of chips. How do I get more?

Tap the chip balance or the "+" and watch a short rewarded video to earn more chips — always free. If you'd like a bigger stack, you can also buy an optional chip pack. You never have to pay to keep playing.

How do I remove the ads?

Any in-app purchase permanently removes the banner ads. Buy a chip pack once and the banners are gone for good — across all your devices signed in to the same Apple ID. Rewarded videos stay available (and free) if you want to earn extra chips.

I bought something but still see ads.

Open Settings → Restore Purchases and make sure you're signed into the Apple ID you bought with. Ad removal syncs through iCloud, so it may take a moment to appear on a second device.

Are the computer opponents fair?

Yes. Deals are random, and each computer player decides using only its own hand and the cards visible on the table — it never peeks at undealt cards. It plays by fixed rules, the same as you.

Which games are included?

Twenty-three: Texas Hold'em, Blackjack, Baccarat, Video Poker, Casino War, Red Dog, War, Gin Rummy, Chinchón, Konken, Hearts, King, Briscola, Brisca, Scopa, Escoba, Pişti, Crazy Eights, Mau-Mau, Pis Yedili, Solitaire, FreeCell, and Spider.

How do I learn a game I don't know?

Open a game and tap the ⓘ for a full how-to-play guide with rules and trivia. New games also show a short intro the first time, and every game has a narrated "Watch & Learn" demo that plays itself — it never costs any chips.

Can I play offline?

Yes — all the games work without a connection. You only need to be online to load an ad or watch a rewarded video for chips.

How do I change the language, colors, or ad-consent choices?

Open Settings. Language and Appearance are near the top; your ad-consent choices are under Settings → About → Privacy choices (shown where consent applies).

I found a bug or have a suggestion.

Please email nae_dev@icloud.com — we read every message and appreciate the feedback.

Nein. PokerMoker dient nur der Unterhaltung. Du spielst mit virtuellen Chips ohne realen Wert — sie lassen sich nicht auszahlen, einlösen oder übertragen, und es gibt keine Geldpreise. Die App ist ab 17 freigegeben, weil sie Casino-Spiele simuliert.

Ja. Die Karten werden zufällig gegeben, und jeder Computer-Spieler entscheidet nur anhand seines eigenen Blatts und der auf dem Tisch sichtbaren Karten — er schaut nie in die ungegebenen Karten. Er spielt nach festen Regeln, genau wie du.
