What is birden?

birden is a number-merge puzzle. Swipe to slide the tiles; when two equal numbers meet, they merge and double. It's the game you know — the classic merge puzzle — with a twist: an enhanced birden mode where tiles spawn as 1, 2, 4 or 8, merges can chain into big cascades, and power-ups let you shape the board. Play the pure classic, the enhanced mode, or the shared Daily Challenge.

Two ways to play

  • Classic — the timeless original, tiles start as 2s and 4s.
  • birden — 1/2/4/8 tiles, optional Chain Merge cascades, and a fixed set of power-ups every game.

Power-ups (birden mode)

  • Undo a move, Swap two tiles, Preview the next tile
  • Hammer to remove any 3 tiles, Blast a 3×3 area, Shuffle the board
  • Plus a Last Chance revive at game over

Daily Challenge

One shared starting board for everyone, every day. One attempt — highest score wins, on a leaderboard that resets daily. Come back each morning for a new puzzle.

Free & fair

  • Free to play, no account, no sign-up — play offline
  • Game Center leaderboards & achievements
  • One banner ad — and a single, optional purchase removes it for good
  • Six languages
