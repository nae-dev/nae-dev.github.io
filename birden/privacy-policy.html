English

Summary

birden is a number-merge puzzle game (a fresh take on 2048) that you play on your own device. There are no accounts, and we run no servers — we collect no personal data of our own. Your game progress stays on your device. The app is free and supported by a banner ad through Google AdMob, and a one-time in-app purchase removes that banner for good. Optional Game Center leaderboards and achievements are handled by Apple.

Who is responsible

birden is made by NAE Dev. For any privacy question, contact nae_dev@icloud.com.

Your game data stays on your device

Your scores, saved games, statistics, language and appearance settings, and other preferences are stored locally on your device. We have no servers and never receive, see, or store this data. Whether you have removed ads is remembered through your App Store account, so a purchase on one device restores on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID.

No account, no sign-up

birden has no login or account of its own. You can start playing immediately, offline if you like.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, birden can submit your scores to leaderboards (including the Daily Challenge) and unlock achievements. Game Center is Apple's service; your player identity and scores are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can play the whole game without Game Center — your scores simply won't appear on the global leaderboards.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free app shows a single banner ad through Google AdMob. birden does not ask to track you and does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) for cross-app tracking, so ads are non-personalized (contextual). To deliver and measure these ads, Google may still process limited technical data such as your IP address, device type and settings, and ad interactions. Google acts as an independent controller of that data under its own Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We never receive your gameplay content or identity through ads.

Removing ads

A one-time in-app purchase permanently removes the banner ad. Once removed, the banner no longer loads on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is made through the App Store using Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment or card details.

Measurement (SKAdNetwork)

The app supports Apple's SKAdNetwork, a privacy-preserving system that lets advertisers measure ad campaigns without identifying you.

Children's privacy

birden is a simple puzzle game suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone.

Security

Your game data is protected by the security of your device (Apple device encryption). Because we store none of your data on our own systems, there is nothing on our side to be exposed.

International data transfers

Google and Apple may process ad, Game Center, and purchase requests on servers in other countries, under their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email nae_dev@icloud.com.
