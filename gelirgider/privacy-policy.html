English

Summary

GelirGider is a personal finance app that you fill in yourself. It collects no personal data on our servers — we don't have any. Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you enable iCloud sync (a premium feature), in your own private iCloud. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics.

Who is responsible

GelirGider is made by NAE Dev. For any privacy question, contact nae_dev@icloud.com.

Your data stays with you

Every ledger, entry, budget, and savings holding you create is stored locally on your device. If you are a premium subscriber and turn on iCloud sync, that data is also stored in your own private iCloud so it can appear on your other devices. This uses Apple's iCloud — we run no servers and never receive, see, or store your financial data.

Manual entry only — no bank access

GelirGider does not connect to your bank, cards, or any financial institution. You type in your own income, expenses, and balances; the app cannot read your real accounts.

Market-price requests (premium)

When you use premium pricing features — foreign-currency conversion, precious metals, cryptocurrencies, or stocks — the app fetches public market prices from third-party providers: Frankfurter and ExchangeRate-API (exchange rates), CoinGecko (crypto), gold-api.com (metals), and Yahoo Finance (stocks). These requests contain only what is needed to look up a price — a currency code, a coin identifier, or a ticker symbol — and never your amounts, balances, or identity. Each provider may log your device's IP address under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Premium is purchased through the App Store using Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment or card details.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

GelirGider has no sign-up, login, or account of its own — iCloud sync uses the Apple ID already on your device, managed by Apple. The app contains no advertising, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party analytics, and it performs no tracking across other apps or websites. Because nothing is tracked, no App Tracking Transparency permission is requested.

Your control

You are always in control. You can export a full backup as a JSON file (premium), and you can permanently erase everything from Settings → Data Transfer → Clear. Deleting the app removes all on-device data; data in your iCloud is removed through Apple when you delete it there.

Data retention

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Your data lives only where you keep it — on your device and, optionally, in your iCloud — for as long as you keep it.

Children's privacy

GelirGider is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Security

Your data is protected by the security of your device and your iCloud account (Apple device encryption). Since we store none of your data on our own systems, there is nothing on our side to be exposed.

International data transfers

The third-party price providers and Apple's iCloud may process requests on servers in other countries. Only the non-personal price-lookup data described above (and, for iCloud, your own data handled by Apple) is involved.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email nae_dev@icloud.com.
